Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

The short version: Mettle stores your email, your interview answers and your scores. Your answers are sent to Anthropic to be graded. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and you can delete all of it from Settings at any time.

2. What data is collected

Account data

  • Email address — so you have an account and can sign back in.
  • Display name and first name, if you sign in with Google and Google provides them. Used only to greet you on the dashboard.
  • A password hash, if you sign up with email and password. The plain password is never seen or stored by Mettle — Supabase handles hashing.

Interview data

  • The questions you were asked and the topic and level you chose.
  • Your answers, as you typed them.
  • Your scores on each answer and for the session overall, plus the notes the grader produced about what a complete answer would have covered.
  • How many interviews you have used, to apply the free-usage limit.

Technical data

  • Standard server logs from hosting providers — IP address, timestamp, and which page or endpoint was requested. Used to keep the service running and diagnose faults.
  • A theme preference and a sign-in session token stored locally in your browser or on your device. These are not sent anywhere.

Mettle has no advertising, no analytics product, and no third-party tracking cookies.

3. Why it is collected

Your data is processed on the basis of your consent, which you give by creating an account and starting an interview, and only for these purposes:

  • Running an interview and generating the next question.
  • Grading your answers and producing your scorecard.
  • Showing your history and your progress over time.
  • Applying the free-usage limit and preventing abuse of the service.
  • Keeping the service secure and working.

Your data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you for any purpose other than showing you your own progress.

4. Your answers are sent to Anthropic for grading

This is the most important thing to understand about how Mettle works. When you submit an answer, the question and the full text of your answer are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the scores and notes you see on your scorecard. The same applies to the previous answer in a session when the interviewer asks a follow-up.

Anthropic processes this data as a service provider in order to return a result. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data submitted through their API is not used to train their models. Anthropic may retain it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. Their policy governs that retention and is linked below.

Do not type anything into an answer that you would not want processed by a third party. Answer as you would in a real interview — there is no reason to include personal details, employer-confidential information, or credentials, and you should not.

5. Who else processes your data

Mettle is built on third-party infrastructure. Each provider below processes data only to deliver its service, and each is located outside India, so using Mettle involves transferring your data abroad. The DPDP Act permits such transfers except to countries the Government of India restricts.

6. How long it is kept

  • Account and interview data — until you delete it. Mettle does not expire or archive your history on its own.
  • Deleting your history (Settings → Clear history) removes every session, answer and score from the database immediately. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) removes your interview data and your account. This cannot be undone.
  • Server logs are retained by the hosting providers under their own retention schedules, typically for a short period.
  • Data already sent to Anthropic is subject to Anthropic's retention policy and cannot be recalled by Mettle after the fact.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Know what is held about you and who it has been shared with. Your interview data is visible in the app under History and Progress; write to the address above for anything else.
  • Correct or complete inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data — available directly in Settings, or by writing to the address above.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. Deleting your account withdraws it in full; after that Mettle holds nothing to process.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity. Write to the address above to register a nominee.
  • Raise a grievance with the Grievance Officer named in section 1. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

  • All traffic between the app, the API and the database uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase. Mettle never receives or stores a plain password.
  • Every API request is authenticated with a signed token verified on the server. Your user identity is read from that verified token, never from anything the app sends.
  • Database row-level security restricts each row to the account that owns it, so one user cannot read another user's interviews.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, you and the Data Protection Board of India will be notified as the DPDP Act requires.

9. Children

Mettle is intended for working software developers and is not for anyone under 18. Accounts are not knowingly created for children, and no behavioural advertising or tracking is directed at anyone. If you believe a child has created an account, write to the address above and it will be deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects how your data is handled, the date at the top will be updated and notice will be given in the app before the change takes effect. Continuing to use Mettle after that constitutes consent to the updated policy.
